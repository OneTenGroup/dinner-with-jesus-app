How it works

Three simple moments. One meaningful dinner.

Open the app at dinner and follow three simple moments together.

1

Read one verse

Tonight’s Scripture comes with just enough context to help everyone understand what’s happening.

2

Start one conversation

Choose a question that fits your table—from curious and simple to honest and deeper.

3

Share one prayer

The words are already there. Take turns, read together, and close the night with prayer.

Make tonight count

The ordinary dinners are the ones that matter.

We never really know how much time we have together. Life moves quickly, children grow, seasons change, and too many meaningful conversations get saved for later.

Dinner with Jesus is a simple invitation to slow down and make the most of tonight—to listen, laugh, talk about what matters, and grow closer to God and one another.

No pressure to make every dinner profound. Just one verse, one conversation, one prayer, and a little more time together.
